# ADR-0076: Separate venue booking hold and decision summaries from details

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** 2026-10-30
- **Related stories:** SCRUM-40, SCRUM-110, SCRUM-121

## Decision

Tentative-hold and decided venue bookings use compact summary cards consistent with coordinator
cards. View details reveals full booking data and existing expiry controls. Pending booking review
remains directly actionable.
